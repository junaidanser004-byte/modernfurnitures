❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about Modern Furnitures.
Orders & Delivery
How long does delivery take? ⌄
Delivery within Qatar takes 3–7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email with your order details once your order is placed.
Can I track my order? ⌄
Yes! Use the Track Order page and enter the Tracking ID from your invoice or confirmation email.
Do you deliver outside Qatar? ⌄
Currently we deliver within Qatar only. We plan to expand to the GCC region soon.
What are the shipping charges? ⌄
We offer free shipping on select orders and a flat rate of QAR 50 for standard delivery. The shipping cost is shown at checkout before you place your order.
Payments
What payment methods do you accept? ⌄
We accept Credit / Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
Is online payment secure? ⌄
Yes. All card payments are processed securely through Stripe, a globally trusted payment provider. We do not store your card details.
Returns & Cancellations
Can I cancel my order? ⌄
You can cancel your order within 24 hours of placing it by contacting us at info@modernfurnitures.qa or calling +974 22334455.
What is your return policy? ⌄
We accept returns within 7 days of delivery if the product is unused and in original condition. Please contact our support team to initiate a return.
Products & Warranty
Do your products come with a warranty? ⌄
Yes, all our products come with a manufacturer warranty ranging from 1 to 3 years depending on the item. Warranty details are listed on each product page.
How do I contact customer support? ⌄
You can reach us via the Contact page, email us at info@modernfurnitures.qa, or call +974 22334455. We're available Saturday–Thursday, 9am–6pm.
