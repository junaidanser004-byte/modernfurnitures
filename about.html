Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Modern Furnitures.

How long does delivery take? ⌄
Delivery within Qatar takes 3–7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once your order is placed.
Can I track my order? ⌄
Yes! Use the Track Order page and enter the Tracking ID from your invoice or confirmation email.
What payment methods do you accept? ⌄
We accept Credit / Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
Can I cancel my order? ⌄
You can cancel within 24 hours of placing it by contacting us at info@modernfurnitures.qa or calling +974 22334455.
Do your products come with a warranty? ⌄
Yes, all products come with a manufacturer warranty of 1–3 years depending on the item. Details are listed on each product page.
What is your return policy? ⌄
We accept returns within 7 days of delivery if the product is unused and in its original condition. Contact our support team to initiate a return.
